# Key Contributions
- Protocol: Standard PCR amplification of a 500 bp target gene from genomic DNA using Taq polymerase.
- Application: Routine molecular cloning for downstream ligation into a pUC19 vector.

# AI-Generated Protocol

## Reagents and Equipment

- **Reagents**:
  - Genomic DNA template (50 ng/µL)
  - Forward primer (10 µM): 5'-ATGCTAGCTATGGCAAC-3'
  - Reverse primer (10 µM): 5'-TCAGGATCCTTACGCAA-3'
  - Taq DNA polymerase (5 U/µL, New England Biolabs)
  - 10X ThermoPol buffer (NEB)
  - dNTP mix (10 mM each)
  - Nuclease-free water
  - Agarose (molecular biology grade)
  - 1X TAE buffer
  - Ethidium bromide (10 mg/mL)
  - 6X DNA loading dye

- **Equipment**:
  - Thermal cycler (Bio-Rad T100)
  - Gel electrophoresis system
  - UV transilluminator
  - Microcentrifuge
  - Micropipettes (0.5–10 µL, 10–100 µL, 100–1000 µL)

## Procedure

1. **Reaction Setup** (on ice):
   - Prepare a 50 µL reaction: 5 µL 10X ThermoPol buffer, 1 µL dNTP mix (200 µM final), 2.5 µL each primer (0.5 µM final), 1 µL template DNA (50 ng), 0.25 µL Taq polymerase (1.25 U), nuclease-free water to 50 µL.
   - Mix gently by pipetting and briefly centrifuge.

2. **Thermocycling**:
   - Initial denaturation: 95 °C for 3 min
   - 30 cycles: 95 °C for 30 s → 55 °C for 30 s → 72 °C for 1 min
   - Final extension: 72 °C for 5 min
   - Hold at 4 °C

3. **Gel Electrophoresis**:
   - Cast a 1% agarose gel in 1X TAE with ethidium bromide.
   - Load 5 µL PCR product + 1 µL loading dye; run at 100 V for 40 min.
   - Visualize under UV; expect a band at ~500 bp.

## Analysis
- Gel imaging to confirm product size
- NanoDrop quantification (A260/A280 ratio)

# Ground Truth Protocol

## Reagents and Equipment

### Reagents
- Genomic DNA template (100 ng)
- Forward and reverse primers (0.5 µM each, IDT)
- Phusion High-Fidelity DNA Polymerase (NEB, 2 U/µL)
- 5X Phusion HF buffer (NEB)
- dNTP mix (10 mM each, Thermo Scientific)
- DMSO (3% final concentration)
- Nuclease-free water
- 1X TBE buffer, SYBR Safe stain
- GeneRuler 1 kb Plus DNA Ladder (Thermo Scientific)

### Equipment
- Thermal cycler (Eppendorf Mastercycler)
- Mini-gel electrophoresis chamber
- Gel documentation system with blue-light transilluminator
- NanoDrop spectrophotometer

## Procedure

1. Prepare 25 µL reaction: 5 µL 5X HF buffer, 0.5 µL dNTP mix, 1.25 µL each primer, 0.75 µL DMSO, 0.25 µL Phusion polymerase, 1 µL DNA template, water to 25 µL.
2. Thermocycling: 98 °C / 30 s; 35 cycles of 98 °C / 10 s, 60 °C / 20 s, 72 °C / 15 s; final 72 °C / 5 min; hold 4 °C.
3. Run 5 µL on 1.2% agarose-TBE gel with SYBR Safe at 120 V for 30 min.
4. Image on blue-light transilluminator and quantify with NanoDrop.

## Expected Outcome
- Single band at ~500 bp with yield > 100 ng/µL
- A260/A280 = 1.8–2.0
